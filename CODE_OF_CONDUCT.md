# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete risks.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar project conventions.
- Protect private data and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports should be made privately to the maintainer and will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
